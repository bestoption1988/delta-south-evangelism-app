❓ Help Center / Member Care

Need help with your membership registration? The information below answers the most common questions. If you need personal assistance, use the dashboard login after your account has been approved.
📋 How do I register? Complete the registration form, select your Circuit and Local Church, create your username and password, then submit the registration.
⏳ What happens after registration? Your registration is initially marked Pending. An authorized church officer must review and approve it.
🆔 Where is my Registration ID? Your Registration ID is displayed after successful submission. Please keep it safely for future reference.
🔐 When can I log in? You can use your login account after your membership registration has been approved by the authorized church officer.
📷 What photo should I upload? Use a clear passport photograph or selfie. JPG, PNG and WebP images are accepted.
✏️ Can I correct my registration? If information needs correction, contact your Local Church office or use Member Care after logging into your account.
🏛️ Who approves my registration? Registration approval is handled by an authorized church officer according to the church's membership administration process.
🆘 I need additional assistance. Use the Login to Dashboard button to enter the membership system and access the available member-care services.
