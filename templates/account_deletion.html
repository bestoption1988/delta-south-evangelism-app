MCN Delta South — Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of an account used in the MCN Delta South app and associated personal data. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to bestoption1988@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line MCN Delta South — Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your account username, the email or phone number linked to the account, and a brief request to delete your account and associated personal data.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Email a deletion request

What happens next?

After verifying the request, the Diocese will review the account and related records, arrange deletion of personal data that can lawfully be deleted, and request deletion from relevant service providers where applicable. We may retain limited records where necessary for legal, financial, audit, security or fraud-prevention obligations. If any information must be retained, the reason and applicable category will be explained.

Submitting an email starts a request; it does not mean that the account or its records have already been deleted. We will respond to the request using the contact details provided.

Need help?

Contact bestoption1988@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords, PINs, full payment-card numbers or bank login details.
