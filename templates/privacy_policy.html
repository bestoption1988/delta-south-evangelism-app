Privacy Policy

App: MCN Delta South — Department of Evangelism
Controller / service operator: Methodist Church Nigeria, Delta South Diocese (app service)
Privacy contact: bestoption1988@gmail.com
Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how the MCN Delta South app and its connected web services handle information. The Android app displays the Diocese's online service, so this policy applies to information submitted through the app and the related website.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and contact information: name, username, phone number, email address, password credentials (stored in protected/hashed form), account role and account status.
  • Church and membership information: circuit, local church, membership details, fellowship and ministry information, dates and other details a member or authorised church officer enters into the service.
  • Photos and documents: profile photographs, leadership photographs, church media, documents and other files uploaded for the service.
  • Requests and communications: prayer requests, member-care messages, enquiries, testimonies, appreciation messages and information supplied in those submissions.
  • Church administration and finance records: donations, giving, pledges, payment references, transaction status and related records entered or generated by the service. Payment-card or bank credentials should be entered only on the payment provider's own secure checkout, not in ordinary app messages.
  • Technical information: basic request, security and diagnostic information that may be recorded by the hosting platform or service providers when the app is used.

Information collected depends on the features a person uses and the information they or an authorised church officer submit.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts; administer church membership and evangelism activities; manage church communications, prayer and member-care requests; display approved church content; process and reconcile giving or payment records; provide support; maintain backups; prevent misuse; and meet applicable church, accounting and legal obligations.

3. Service providers and sharing

Information is accessible to authorised Diocese/church personnel according to their assigned roles. The service may use infrastructure and specialist providers to operate its features, including the Naijacloud hosting environment, Supabase storage/database services where configured, payment processors selected and configured by the Diocese, and an AI service for messages submitted to the optional AI member-care assistant. Those providers process relevant information to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices. The particular payment provider depends on the payment settings active at the time of a transaction.

We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect users or the service, or when a user asks us to share it.

4. Photos, media and public content

Some church media, leadership profiles, event information and other materials may be deliberately published for public viewing. Private membership, account and payment records are not intended for public display. Please do not upload information or images unless you are authorised to do so.

5. Security

The service uses HTTPS for its public web connection and applies account authentication and role-based access controls. Passwords are handled using password hashing. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; users should protect their passwords and notify the Diocese promptly if they suspect unauthorised access.

6. Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, maintain church membership and administrative history, reconcile financial records, preserve required audit trails and backups, resolve disputes, and meet legal or legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Retention periods can differ by record type and by applicable obligations. Backups and copies held by service providers may take additional time to expire after an approved deletion.

7. Access, correction and deletion requests

You may ask to access or correct information associated with your account, or request deletion of your app account and associated personal data. Visit the Account and Data Deletion Request page or email bestoption1988@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account where possible. Include the app name, your account username, and a way for us to verify your request. Do not send your password, PIN, full card number or bank login details.

We will verify the requester and assess the request. Where deletion is approved, we will arrange deletion of data that we control and request deletion by relevant service providers where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate legal, financial, security, fraud-prevention or audit purposes; if so, we will explain the applicable retention reason. Sending a request does not itself mean deletion has already occurred.

8. Children

The service is intended for church administration and community use, not as a child-directed consumer app. Where information about a child is entered for church purposes, it should be supplied by an authorised parent, guardian or church officer and limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions, access/correction requests or deletion requests, contact bestoption1988@gmail.com.

This notice describes the service based on its current features and configuration. The Diocese should review it whenever a payment provider, storage provider, AI feature or data practice changes.
